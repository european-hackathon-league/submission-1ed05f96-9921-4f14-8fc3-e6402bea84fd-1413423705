Brand sheet

Dixi is named after the Dixi 3/15, BMW's first car (1928). Colours inspired by BMW's public brand and BMW M colours; never the BMW logo.

Logo

A globe crossed by a decision track: signal (light blue) → evidence (BMW blue) → human sign-off (M red).

Three layers: depth of blue = strength of signal

L1

Principles. Hard rules per market, owned by managers.

L2

Evidence. Customer studies, owner feedback, sales, quality reports.

L3

Signals. Social media, search trends, concept tests. Low weight.
