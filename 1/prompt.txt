# Story

A product film sells **what the product lets a person do and feel**, not what it is. Everything
downstream (look, music, shots, effects) is chosen to serve the beats written here. Fixing a story
in text costs nothing; fixing it after generation costs a render.

## 1. Find the one feeling
Write one sentence: *"A person who ___ feels ___ because the product ___."*
For a photo-library app: *"A mother who can't find her daughter's first day at the sea feels it
again in seconds, because she just asks her phone."* If you can't write it, don't write shots yet.

## 2. The non-negotiables
1. **Show what it does for the person.** Never list features, and never name the technology on
   screen ("on-device model" becomes "Your photos never leave your phone").
2. **A human mini-movie:** a person, a small real problem, the product solving it quietly, and an
   emotional payoff that ties back to a human experience.
3. **Music and sound carry the emotion.** Voice-over is minimal: 0–4 short lines. The video talks.
4. **Show, don't tell.** The phone is in a hand, in a real place (sofa, kitchen, train). The UI
   appears because someone uses it.
5. **The product moment must be unmistakable.** The single clearest thing in the film is the user
   *doing* the key action: typing the query into the search bar, and the right photo appearing. The
   user rated this "really clear what it does". Use the real UI (see `../06-effects`), never a
   generated screen.
6. **One idea per title card.** "10,000 photos." / "One sentence." / "Found."

## 3. Structure (adapt the lengths)

| beat | 60 s | 120 s | job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–5 | 0–10 | an image or a line that makes you lean in |
| World / problem | 5–15 | 10–35 | the person and the small frustration (endless scrolling) |
| Turn | 15–30 | 35–60 | the key action, shown exactly (types the query → results) |
| Payoff montage | 30–45 | 60–100 | 2–4 human moments the product gave back |
| Trust | 45–52 | 100–115 | one line of reassurance (privacy), a held breath |
| Close | 52–60 | 115–120 | logo, tagline, one warm last image |

Other structures live in the repo's `frameworks/` and `playbooks/` (H-S-T-S-S, the Pixar formula,
Apple beat analyses). Pick one on purpose.

## 4. Write three artefacts

**a. Beat sheet** (`story/beats.md`): for each beat, the time range, the emotion (one word), what
we see, what we hear (music level, SFX, the line if any), and the **story requirement**: the one
thing that must be true on screen for the beat to work, e.g. *"her face shows relief, not a
smile-for-camera"*. These requirements become validation gates (`../08-validation`).

**b. Script** (`story/script.md`): the spoken lines and the title cards, with timings.
Word budget per spoken line = `floor(seconds × 2.3) − 1` (a calm narrator reads ~140 wpm, and a
line needs air at both ends).

**c. Shot list** (`story/shots.json`): one entry per shot:
```json
{"id": "s04", "beat": "turn", "seconds": 5, "kind": "ui|generated|hybrid|title",
 "first_frame": "what the still shows (subject, place, framing) — no motion words",
 "motion": "the one thing that moves", "camera": "one move: static|push_in|pan_left|…",
 "cast": ["mother"], "ui": "search-typing: 'the beach with the kids'", "effect": "grid-to-memory-match-cut",
 "sound": "key taps, soft room tone", "line": "", "requirements": ["query reads exactly …"]}
```
`kind: ui` shots are rendered from real screenshots; `generated` from a model; `hybrid` composites
a real UI into a generated shot (product-in-hand).

## 5. Check the story before spending anything
- Can you tell the story from the titles and lines alone? From the pictures alone?
- Is the key product action on screen for ≥ 3 s, readable, and with no cut inside it?
- Does every shot earn its seconds? Cut the one you like least.
- Is there exactly one clear emotional peak, and does the music plan (`../03-music`) put its
  swell there?
- Recurring people: declared once as `cast`, with the same description everywhere (`../02-look-and-cast`).

Worked examples: `new-skills/h3-remotion-typography/story/` (user-liked: the typed query
"Mum laughing" → results → match cut into the memory) and `new-skills/h3-reference-motion/story/story.md`.
